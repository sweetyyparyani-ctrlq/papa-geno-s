Our Culinary Heritage

The Secret Behind Papa Geno's Flavor

At Papa Geno's, we believe great food begins with uncompromised ingredients and time-honored craft. We bridge authentic Italian stone-baking methods with Pakistan's most beloved smoky tandoor spices — resulting in an unforgettable flavor explosion.

48-Hour Cold Ferment

Our sourdough crust rises slowly for 48 hours for an airy, crisp crumb that is light on the stomach.

100% Mozzarella

Never processed cheese blends. We shred fresh whole-milk mozzarella for the iconic golden cheese pull.

Clay-Oven Tikka & Bihari

Real boneless chicken marinated in yogurt, raw papaya, and roasted masalas for true melt-in-mouth tenderness.

Fresh Farm Produce

Crisp red peppers, vine tomatoes, and fresh herbs handpicked every morning from local farmers.
